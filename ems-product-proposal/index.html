Building owners pay for energy they cannot see.

01 · The problem
22%

of the heat pumps' electricity in our own pilot building went to pumps and control circuits. Nobody could see it until we put a meter on each circuit.

99% of hours

The heat pump's own “cooling hours” counter ticked almost every hour, whether it was cooling or not. The building's built-in figures can't be trusted on their own.

Source: our pilot building at VATP, Ventspils, 14 to 30 days of metered data05
02 · The product

Measure every machine. Then make it use less.

A system that shows where a building's energy goes, machine by machine and hour by hour, and then runs the heating, ventilation and heat pumps to waste less of it.

Where the energy goes06
02 · The product

How it works

Measure

Meters and sensors

On every heat pump, air handler, pump circuit and room. Most buildings already have equipment we can read.

Collect

A server in the building

Data stays on site and the system keeps working without internet.

Show

Screens and reports

kWh, euros and CO₂ per machine, per hour. Alerts when something breaks.

Control · next step

Automatic control

Schedules, setpoints and heat pumps run to use less, within safety limits.

Control is built in the pilot, not yet switched on07
