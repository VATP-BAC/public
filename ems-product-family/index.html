Why the products belong together

One data thread from first estimate to proven CO₂ cut

Product 1Estimated

A first figure from a few inputs on the website.

Product 2Baseline

Real use by end use, with the CO₂ footprint.

Product 3Measured

The baseline confirmed or corrected by hourly data.

Product 4Verified

Savings proven against the same baseline, month after month.

actual   = Σ kWh in hour × price in that hour
baseline = Σ kWh that day × mean price that day
saving   = baseline − actual

The pilot already works this way for hot water scheduling. Savings are calculated from source data on every read, so anyone can check them by hand. Swap the hourly price for the hourly grid emission factor and the same method proves CO₂ reduction, the figure funding calls score.
